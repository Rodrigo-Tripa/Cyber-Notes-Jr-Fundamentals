# Anonymity & Privacy

The `11-Anonymity` directory contains supplementary material focused on anonymity, privacy, operational security, threat modeling, browser and endpoint privacy, anonymity networks, and traffic analysis.

This directory is **not required for the essential study of cybersecurity**. The concepts and techniques documented here are not prerequisites for building a fundamental cybersecurity knowledge base, and progress through the main learning structure does not depend on completing these notes.

The purpose of this section is to provide an additional area of study for anyone interested in understanding the relationship between cybersecurity, privacy, anonymity, and operational security. These subjects become particularly relevant when studying areas such as privacy-preserving technologies, threat modeling, intelligence gathering, network analysis, anti-tracking mechanisms, or high-risk operational environments.

The notes in this directory should therefore be treated as **optional supplementary knowledge** rather than part of the core cybersecurity curriculum. They can be studied independently and revisited as deeper knowledge in networking, operating systems, web technologies, and security is acquired.
